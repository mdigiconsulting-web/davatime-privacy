💊 DavaTime — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

DavaTime is a free medicine reminder app created by MDigi Consulting. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

DavaTime does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to any server. All data you enter — including medicine names, dosage, alarm times, profile name, and medicine strip photos — is stored locally on your device only.

Camera Access

The app requests camera access solely for photographing medicine strips. These photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared.

Notifications

DavaTime uses local notifications to remind you to take medicines and drink water. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and no notification data is sent to any server.

Advertising

DavaTime displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for more details:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy

Third Party Services

This app uses the following third party services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

Children's Privacy

DavaTime is designed for adults and elderly users. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
